## Design Context

### Users
Deree students and club creators use Universe to discover campus events, join the community, publish activities, find opportunities, and reserve facilities on desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality
Welcoming, clear, trustworthy. A finished campus portal with understandable actions and consistent feedback.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the original Universe blue-and-white identity, existing typefaces, artwork, and familiar page structure. Repair layout and interaction problems rather than redesigning the product.

### Design Principles
- Use one coherent layout and component system across public pages.
- Make every page useful and readable on phones as well as wide screens.
- Show accurate content, purposeful imagery, and explicit demo disclosures.
- Preserve functional behavior, user activity, accessibility, and administrator changes.
